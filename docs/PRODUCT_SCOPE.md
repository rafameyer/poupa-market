# Product Scope

## Goal

PoupaMarket helps users plan grocery shopping lists and compare estimated totals across nearby markets to understand where they can save money.

## Planned capabilities

- Create weekly, biweekly, and monthly shopping lists
- Maintain reusable product and market data
- Record price entries by product and market
- Compare list totals across markets
- Highlight possible savings

## Explicitly out of scope for Phase 0

- Product CRUD
- Market CRUD
- Price CRUD
- Shopping list CRUD
- Real comparison logic
- OCR
- Scraping
- Payments
- Supabase integration
- Real authentication
- Push notifications
- Complex backend services

## Product principles

- Mobile-first interaction on iPhone and iPad
- Local-first behavior for the MVP
- Simple deployment to Vercel
- Code structure that is easy to extend with Codex
